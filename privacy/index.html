Pocket Plan Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026 · Applies to the Pocket Plan app for Android · Terms of Service

Pocket Plan is a personal budgeting app. It is built so that your money data stays on your phone. We (the developer of Pocket Plan) do not run servers for the app, do not have user accounts, and do not receive your data.

Data stored on your phone

The app stores the following only on your device, in a database encrypted with AES-256 (SQLCipher) whose key is protected by the Android Keystore:

  • Income and expense entries you add (amount, date, description, merchant, category, account).
  • Your accounts: nickname, bank or e-wallet name, account holder name, account number and balance.
  • Your split plan, categories and transfer checklist.
  • Your settings, such as currency and app lock preferences.

AI provider API keys you enter are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key held in the Android Keystore (in secure hardware where the phone has it). Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for this data.

Data sent to the AI provider you choose

Pocket Plan's AI features work by sending requests directly from your phone to the AI provider you select and authorize with your own API key: Google (Gemini), OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude) or OpenRouter. We are not involved in these requests and cannot see them. When you use the assistant, the request can include:

WhatWhen
Your message, or the text recognised from your voiceWhenever you send something in the assistant
Voice recordingsOnly for voice entries with Gemini or OpenAI. With Claude and OpenRouter, speech is converted to text on your phone first.
Photos you attach (receipts, screenshots)Only when you attach or share one. Photos are re-saved before upload, which removes location and other metadata.
Category names and account nicknamesWith every request, so entries can be filed correctly
A money summary: account balances, monthly totals, your 40 most recent entries, detected recurring payments and flagsOnly if "Let the advisor read my history" is on (Settings › Security & privacy). It is on by default and can be turned off.

Never sent: account numbers, account holder names and your API keys (except to the provider the key belongs to, to authenticate).

How each provider handles this data is governed by its own terms and privacy policy, which you agree to when you create your API key. Requests use HTTPS, and the app does not trust user-installed certificates.

Things that happen only on your phone

  • Spotting patterns and insights (spending streaks, budgets running hot, upcoming bills, paydays).
  • Daily check-in notifications, if you turn them on. These are worked out on the device. No AI request is made, and notifications show no amounts on the lock screen.
  • CSV import and password-protected backup files, which are written only where you choose to save them.

Permissions

  • Microphone: only while you hold the mic button to record an entry.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on daily check-ins.
  • Internet: only to reach the AI provider you chose.
  • Camera and photos: Pocket Plan uses the system camera and photo picker, so it only receives the single photo you take or pick.
  • Biometrics / device lock: to unlock the app if you turn on app lock. Pocket Plan never sees your fingerprint or PIN.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data. We never receive it.
  • No moving of money. Pocket Plan is a budgeting tool, not a bank or payment service.

Your control and deletion

You can edit or delete any entry, remove your API keys, turn off history sharing, or erase everything in Settings › Import & backup › Erase all data. Uninstalling the app also deletes all of its data from your phone. Data you sent to an AI provider is handled under that provider's retention policy; contact the provider to request deletion there.

Children

Pocket Plan is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
